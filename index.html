OUR STORY & VALUES

Great Coffee, Honest Food & Care.

Founded in 2018 in Dhaka, Velvet Brew started with a simple passion: serve delicious specialty coffee and honest food made from scratch every day. We partner directly with small family farms in Colombia and Ethiopia to bring you fair-trade beans of the highest quality.

Every morning at dawn, our bakers prepare fresh sourdough bread using natural ingredients and a slow 36-hour fermentation for rich flavor and easy digestion.

🌿

Eco-Friendly Practices

We use compostable cups, recyclable packaging, and turn our coffee grounds into organic garden compost.

🔥

Fresh Weekly Roasting

We roast our beans in small batches every week to highlight natural notes of chocolate, caramel, and fruit.
