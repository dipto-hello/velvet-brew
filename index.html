OUR CRAFT & VALUES

Specialty Sourcing & Transparent Craft

Velvet Brew was established on Road 11, Banani to make single-origin coffee culture approachable and transparent in Dhaka. We work directly with smallholder producer cooperatives in Huila, Colombia, paying well above Fair-Trade minimums to support sustainable farming and ensure exceptional bean quality.

Our bakery follows traditional European fermentation methods—using natural wild sourdough starters and slow 36-hour cold proofing to develop open crumb texture and complex grain flavor without artificial improvers.

Direct-Origin Provenance

Every coffee lot includes verified harvest elevation, washing process, and roast date marked transparently on each retail bag.

Plant-Based & Low-Waste

Takeaway cups and lids are 100% plant-based compostable, and used espresso grounds are collected weekly for local organic composting.
